About me

Engineer by training, builder by instinct.

I'm a Computer Science Engineering undergrad at Vimal Jyoti Engineering College, and I like problems that sit at the intersection of people and systems.

Most of my work starts the same way: someone describes a painful manual process, and I go build the thing that removes it. That's how StudentsConnect came about — a hostel leave system replacing paper slips with QR verification, role-based approvals and automatic SMS to parents. It's also why I keep coming back to automation and ML: a 96%-accurate spam classifier wired into an n8n pipeline quietly does a job nobody wanted to do by hand.

I'm comfortable across the stack — Flutter on the front, Django REST and FastAPI on the back, scikit-learn and NLP where the interesting parts live. Right now I'm looking for a team where I can keep shipping and learn from engineers who are better than me.
